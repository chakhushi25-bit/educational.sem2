Explain quantum entanglement in simple terms.

smart_toy

Imagine you have a pair of magic dice. No matter how far apart you take them—even to opposite sides of the universe—when you roll one and it shows a 6, the other one will instantly show a 6 too.

In quantum physics, entanglement is like that. Two particles become connected so that the state of one instantly affects the state of the other, regardless of distance. Albert Einstein famously called this "spooky action at a distance."
