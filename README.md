## 📖 About the Project

**StudySync AI 2.0** is an intelligent academic study planner developed as part of my Artificial Intelligence subject assessment.

The project aims to make academic planning easier, smarter, and more organized with the help of AI-based concepts and technology.

It focuses on helping students manage their academic activities, organize their studies, and develop a more structured approach towards learning.

The main objective of this project is to demonstrate how Artificial Intelligence can be used in the education sector to improve students' academic planning and productivity.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- To demonstrate the application of Artificial Intelligence in education.
- To make academic planning more organized and efficient.
- To explore the role of intelligent technology in student learning.
- To develop a user-friendly academic planning platform.
- To encourage smarter and more structured study habits.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Academic Study Planning:** Designed to support organized academic preparation.
- **AI-Based Approach:** Demonstrates the application of AI concepts in educational planning.
- **Student-Friendly Interface:** Provides an accessible platform for academic planning.
- **Productivity Focus:** Encourages better organization of academic activities.
